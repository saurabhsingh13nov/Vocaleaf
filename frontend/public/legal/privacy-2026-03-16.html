Privacy Policy

Version date: 2026-03-16

Vocaleaf stores account data, child profiles, story metadata, and private asset metadata in order to generate personalized storybooks.

Voice data

Raw voice samples are treated as sensitive, stored privately, and are not exposed through public URLs.

Usage tracking

We record product usage such as stories created, generated images, narration characters, and successful voice clones to enforce plan limits and understand provider costs.

Retention

Deleting a voice profile or story removes product metadata and triggers cleanup of associated private assets where supported.
